# PATTERNS.md — the learnings ledger

Tiered on purpose. A pattern measured on somebody else's account is a prior, not a law.

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LAW** | Obey by default. Breaking one needs a written reason in the draft frontmatter. |
| **BURNED** | Never do this. Each entry names the measurement that burned it. |
| **CANDIDATE** | Observed once, not confirmed. Treat as a strong prior. |
| **INHERITED** | Ported from another account. Craft standard, not evidence about yours. |

Promote a CANDIDATE only when this account's own numbers support it.

---

## LAWS

### P-001 · The hook is the lever, not the CTA
Line one decides reach. Draft five hooks and ship the painfully obvious one.

### P-005 · One belief per post
Two arguments in one post is zero arguments.

### P-011 · Name a specific entity, never a group
"A mid market finance team" is nobody. Specificity is what makes a reader self select,
subject to every naming rule in `VERIFIED-CLAIMS.md`.

### P-012 · Rotate the shape every post
No two consecutive posts share a shape. The feed habituates to structure, not topic.

### P-016 · One unique CTA keyword per post
Uppercase, quoted once, unused in the cooldown window. Reused keywords make replies
impossible to attribute.

### P-019 · One pain per post
Named before drafting, taken from the pain corpus in the ICP's own words.

### P-021 · Cadence ceiling
About one post a day. Volume past that trains the audience to skip you.

### P-023 · Pain-first body
Open on concrete lived pain before any product mention. A hook plus a list is a
rejected draft.

### Two-scoreboard law
Reach and conversion come from different shapes. Decide which one this post is for
before choosing the card, and judge it on that scoreboard only.

### Audience-fit gate
Every prestige anchor, salary figure and named brand must be one the actual ICP pays
for or reports to. Borrowed status anchors from another market read as noise.

### Never-invent law
No customer, logo, result number, testimonial or scarcity figure that is not sourced.
If a brief demands one, refuse the brief and reshape it to the nearest honest post.

### One-door law
Nothing is a draft until it lives in `drafts/`. No parallel folders of half posts.

### Standing-gate law
Before riding any news peg, name a credible, non manufactured reason this account can
speak on it. "It is about AI and we do AI" is manufactured relevance. Kill it.

## BURNED

### B-003 · Never frame a post as an even-handed comparison
"X versus Y, the honest split" dilutes the grievance. Pick one enemy.

### B-005 · Never name five or more brands
The status anchor stops anchoring and the post reads as spectacle. The integration list
is the classic trap. Cap the visible count, enforced by `C-NAMECOUNT`.

### B-007 · Never rerun a skeleton
Never rerun a post structure already fired in this window with only the incumbent or a
reword swapped. Measured collapse on one account running a single skeleton with a fresh
logo each time: 48,919 then 22,417 then 10,864 then 1,743 impressions. Enforced by
`C-SKELETON`.

### B-008 · Never build the hook on a regulation
A regulation citation in line one pulls the compliance-peer room, not buyers. Keep
security as a mid body beat.

### B-009 · Never bury an incumbent whose defenders outnumber its payers in the room
If your ICP resells the thing you are burying, you have pointed a gun at your own
channel. Attack the belief the buyer holds instead of the product the partner sells.

### B-010 · Never put one person's cadence in another person's mouth
A ported voice is a craft standard, not a voice. Signature lines and reframes belonging
to another operator may not appear. Enforced by `C-BORROWED`.

### B-011 · Never repeat a claim primary sources have disproved
Add each disproved wording to `dead_claims` in `config.json` with the date you checked.
The most checkable sentence in a post is the one aimed at an audience that resells the
product you are describing. Enforced by `C-DEADCLAIM`.

### B-012 · Never post at your own house length in somebody else's voice
Word range, emoji ceiling and closer style come from their measured profile, not from
the agency's habits. Enforced by `C-WORDS`, `C-EMOJI`, `C-NO-LADDER`, `C-CLOSE`.
