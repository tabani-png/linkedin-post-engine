# VOICE-PROFILE — <name>

## STATUS: UNVERIFIED

While this line says UNVERIFIED, `C-VOICE` hard blocks every draft. That is deliberate.
Writing in somebody's voice before measuring it produces confident forgery.

To fill this in:

1. Pull 8 or more of their real posts as JSON: `[{"text": ..., "reactions": ...}, ...]`.
2. `python3 scripts/ingest_voice.py posts.json --name "<name>" --slug founder`
3. Read five of the posts yourself and write the register notes below, then change the
   status line to MEASURED.

## The measured profile

Generated by `scripts/ingest_voice.py`. Do not hand edit the numbers.

## What a counter cannot see

Write these yourself after reading the posts:

- **Register.** Formal or fast-typed? Are typos left in? Random title case?
- **Structure.** Do long posts follow a repeatable spine?
- **Closers.** Exact wording of their usual ask.
- **Tells.** Running jokes, recurring words, punctuation habits.

## Sources to model on, and sources not to

**Use:** their own posts on the platform being written for.

**Do not use:** ghostwritten bylines and editor-sanitised columns, the company page
(that is a marketing team, not them), and any other operator's voice DNA. A ported
voice is a craft standard, not a voice, and `C-BORROWED` blocks its signature lines.
