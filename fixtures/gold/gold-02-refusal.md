I was asked to write a customer win post this week.

I am not going to, and the reason is more useful than the post would have been.

We are on early access. The companies trying Northwind are doing it quietly, and none of them have signed off on being named or on a number I could publish. So a win post would mean inventing a logo or inventing a result, and neither is worth your trust.

What I can tell you is the pain we keep hearing, in their words.

A decision gets made on a call and three people remember it three ways. The follow up sits in a thread nobody owns. The person who knows the process is the process.

That is what we built for. Capture what got decided and who owns it, answer from your own documents, run the onboarding.

It runs inside your own AWS or Azure. Your knowledge stays yours.

When a pilot team is happy to be named, I will name them, with their number, not mine.
