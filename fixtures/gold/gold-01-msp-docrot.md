Most ops leads bought an AI seat and think the company remembers things now.

That is the mistake.

Here is what actually happens when the one person who knows everything takes a week off.

The onboarding checklist was in their head. The reason you switched invoicing rules in March was in a thread. The client who always needs a heads up before renewal, also in their head.

Nobody wrote any of it down, because writing it down was never anyone's job.

We keep hearing the same thing from ops teams. The tools were never the problem. The gap between them was.

So we built Northwind to sit in that gap. It captures what got decided, who owns it, and what is due, off the conversation as it happens. It answers from your own documents. It runs onboarding start to finish.

It deploys inside your own AWS or Azure, so the knowledge stays on your side of the wall.
