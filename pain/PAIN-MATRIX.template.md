# PAIN-MATRIX.md — the ICP's pains, in the ICP's own words

One pain per post. Never recycle a pain while a post on it is under 7 days old.
`pain` x `shape` is the whole variety engine.

**Every row must carry a verbatim quote and a live source URL.** A pain described in
your words instead of theirs is the difference between a post that gets recognised and
a post that gets scrolled. A quote here without a URL is worse than no quote.

## STATUS: EMPTY — harvest before drafting

engagement number, it tells you how much of the room agrees.
